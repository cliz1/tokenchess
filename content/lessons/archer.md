@note Capture the pawns.

The Archer is a slow piece, moving one square diagonally at a time. This makes it a color-bound piece, like the Bishop.

```challenge
fen: "4k3/8/5X2/4p3/3pP3/3P4/8/4K3 w - - 0 1"
line: f6e5 e8d8 e5d4
```

---

@note Capture the pieces in the following order: Knight, Rook, Pawn

The Archer can capture without moving, given that the captured piece is **2 to 3 diagonal squares away.**

```challenge
fen: "n3k3/7p/8/8/4X3/8/6r1/1n2K3 w - - 0 1"
line: e4b1 e8d8 e4g2 d8e8 e4h7
```
