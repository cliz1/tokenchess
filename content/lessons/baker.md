@note White to move, mate in two.

A game of chess does not hinge on the survival of a humble Baker, but otherwise they are identical to the King. 
 They are considered more valuable in endgames, and unlike a bishop or knight, they can deliver checkmate alone.

@note Use the Baker to block the checks.
