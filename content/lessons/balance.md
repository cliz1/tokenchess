May 2026: 
 **1) Wizard token cost 4 -> 7**. 
 Fairy stockfish has revealed the tactical potency of this piece, and will consistently favor it over a Rook and Pawn. 
 Don't buy it? Give Fairy stockfish a Wizard and find out! 
 **2) Archer token cost 7 -> 5** 
 While this piece seems unbeatable at first glance, it takes some effort to prove its value. In an endgame, this piece becomes a lot worse, and due to its low range it is likely worth no more than a rook. But certainly not more than two bishops! 
 **3) Snare token cost 2 -> 1** 
 The snare cannot capture, and is very weak to attack by pawns. Giving it a valuation of more than a pawn seems unfair for this reason. 
 **4) Rolling Snare Movement buff** 
 The Rolling Snare now moves like a Queen to make it more comparable to pawn/painter promotions.
