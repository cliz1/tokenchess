@note White to move, mate in one.

The Princess combines the movement of the Bishop and Knight. 
 The combination of short and long range strength makes her exceedingly tricky to play against.

```challenge
fen: "7k/1b6/8/3p4/2p3I1/5P2/r3PK2/8 w - - 0 1"
line: g4f6
```

---

@note Find the checkmate in two. (Difficulty: Medium)

As seen in the above example, one advantage of the Princess is her ability to checkmate a cornered king without additional help. Below is a slightly more complex illustration of this idea.

```challenge
fen: "8/8/8/8/8/7p/5I1k/4K3 w - - 0 1"
```
