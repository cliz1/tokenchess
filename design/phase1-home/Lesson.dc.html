While similar to a pawn, a Painter has no violence in their heart. They are a dreamer, but not a fighter. Rather than capture, the Painter paints pieces the opposite color.

To prevent standoffs between two painters controlling the same square, Painters follow the wet paint rule: once a piece is painted, it cannot be painted again on the opponent's next move. The piece may only be painted again once the paint dries one turn later.
